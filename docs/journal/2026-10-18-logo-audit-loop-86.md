## Current State

- `tools/anatomy.py` defines the reference's anatomy on `snakes/ref/reference.png` only: 18 numbered features H1, L01–L16 and H2 along the W from the yellow head to the blue head, each seeded at a fixed image point, from the saturated blue and yellow pixels split into connected components (tools/anatomy.py:1-13,25-56,59-86; audit/README.md:7-9)
- Two yellow components of the reference each hold two touching lobes (L04 with L06 in the left valley, L12 with L14 in the right valley), which `tools/anatomy.py` splits along the crease between the lobes by a watershed on the smoothed luminance seeded at the lobes' centres (tools/anatomy.py:70-86; audit/README.md:16)
- `tools/anatomy.py` also derives the negative spaces N1–N6 (background components inside the W's convex hull) and writes `audit/registry.json` (per feature: colour, bbox, centroid, area, neighbours; 18 features and 6 negatives), `audit/registry-labels.png`, `audit/registry-negatives.png` and `audit/reference-annotated.png` (tools/anatomy.py:87-130,131-154; audit/registry.json)
- `tools/audit_render.py TAG spec.json|- OUTDIR` renders one candidate through the real scene — colour through `tools/spline_batch.mjs`, clay and zebra (8 bars) through `tools/diag_still.mjs` for the whole frame and for every registry feature's window (bbox padded by 30 px, each window with a separate camera), and the visible fold and self-penetration points of `tools/mesh_check.mjs --dump`; `-` renders the old body; the look is read from `snakes/w-final.look.json` (tools/audit_render.py:1-36)
- `tools/mesh_check.mjs` takes `--dump FILE` and writes the visible fold points `[x, y, area]` and visible self-penetration points to FILE (tools/mesh_check.mjs:18-20,100,114,127)
- `tools/evaluate.py colour.png [--clay] [--folds] [--out]` measures a real render against the registry feature by feature and never uses the JAX surrogate: IoU, centroid offset, area ratio, extremal offsets, boundary distance (mean, p95), signed boundary error per side, colour coverage, merged reference lobes, segmentation-validity flags, clay crease (99th percentile of |Laplacian|) and visible fold area (tools/evaluate.py:1-34,94-198)
- Candidate lobes in `tools/evaluate.py` are split from each colour mask by a watershed on the smoothed distance transform with markers at the h-maxima (h = 5 px); the docstring and `audit/README.md` state that the rule reproduces the reference registry with all 18 features at IoU ≥ 0.97 (tools/evaluate.py:4-10,68-73; audit/README.md:24)
- `tools/evaluate.py` flags a feature `merged`, `split` or `unstable` (the match's IoU moves by more than 0.1 when h is 3 or 8), and the docstring of the file states that a flagged feature's severity may be a segmentation artefact (tools/evaluate.py:19-26,160-171)
- `severity()` in `tools/evaluate.py` takes the worst of three 0–5 scales — IoU (thresholds .85, .75, .65, .5, .35), p95 boundary distance (6, 10, 16, 24, 35 px) and centroid offset (4, 8, 14, 20, 30 px) — and sets at least 4 for a merged lobe and 5 for a missing one; clay crease and fold area do not enter `severity()` (tools/evaluate.py:76-91)
- The global measures of `tools/evaluate.py` are silhouette IoU, silhouette boundary distance (mean, p95, max), blue and yellow IoU, and the intrusion of the silhouette into each negative space (tools/evaluate.py:181-197)
- `tools/critic_pages.py RENDERS_DIR TAG OUT_DIR` writes the pages for an absolute critic — page 0 with reference, candidate colour, clay, zebra and candidate with the reference silhouette outlined in cyan, then six features per page with the same five columns — carrying feature IDs and column names only, with no metrics, history or candidate name (tools/critic_pages.py:1-8,24,60-83)
- `tools/pair_pages.py` writes the pages for a paired judge — reference | X colour | Y colour | X clay | Y clay | X zebra | Y zebra for the whole frame and every feature — with the X/Y order given by the caller (tools/pair_pages.py:1-5,18,53-66)
- `tools/ledger.py ITERDIR --rank TAG [--against BEST]` reads `metrics-<tag>.json`, `mapping.json` (tag to critic letter) and the severity cells of `critic-<letter>.md`, ranks features by metric severity plus critic severity (ties by IoU), and writes `ITERDIR/ledger.md` (tools/ledger.py:1-14,20-35,42-69)
- The gates in `tools/ledger.py` fail a candidate against the best when the metric severity of any feature rises by 2 or more, the IoU of a feature falls by more than 0.08, the critic severity of a feature rises by 2 or more, or the silhouette IoU falls by more than 0.01; fold area and clay crease are not gate inputs (tools/ledger.py:7-9,70-89)
- `tools/who.py spec.json [F1,F2,...]` assigns each pixel of each registry feature's mask the strand section in front (a hard z-buffer of sections with screen half-width r·ql and depth r·qd) and the sections hidden behind, and prints per feature the visible strand with the shared-station range (5th–95th percentile) and the fraction of the mask the strand fills, and the strands passing behind (tools/who.py:1-7,33-60)
- `tools/regionfit.py` gains `--w-feat`, `--targets` and `--target-boost`: for each registry feature whose bbox lies inside the image window, one minus the soft Dice of blue and of yellow inside the feature's window (bbox padded by 20 px), averaged with weights that multiply target features by `--target-boost` (default 4.0) (tools/regionfit.py:32-35,110-112,244-270,409,433)
- `tools/regionfit.py` gains `--order "B:s0-s1>Y:s0-s1@F"` (repeatable), `--order-margin` (30) and `--w-zorder` (2.0): a hinge that puts the first strand section in front of the second by the margin where the two overlap on screen inside reference feature F's mask, and removes the two sections from the crossing-order pairs (tools/regionfit.py:42-47,113-115,348-374,383-388,429)
