## Current State

- `scene.js` frames 1448 x 1086 world units at one unit per reference pixel — a still of that size lines up pixel for pixel with the reference image `snakes/ref/reference.png` (snakes/scene.js:1-4,8)
- `createScene` places an orthographic camera at elevation `params.cam.elev` (3.7746862033886837 degrees in `snakes/w-final.params.json`) looking at the frame centre from 6000 units along (0, sin elev, cos elev), and `resize` keeps the reference framing letterboxed to the canvas aspect (snakes/scene.js:186-191,387-395)
- `centrelines(p, n = 3000)` derives the baseline spine from `p.polyline` (a spine read off the reference) or, without `p.polyline`, from a clamped cubic B-spline through `p.spine`, resamples the spine by arc length, displaces the spine in the picture plane by the piecewise-linear `p.spine_offsets`, and attaches a parallel-transport frame (snakes/geometry.js:64-85)
- `centrelines` integrates the twist angle θ from `p.rate` and, when anchors are present, replaces θ by a monotone cubic (Fritsch-Carlson) through the `anchor_xy` lobe centres projected onto the spine, with a rounded-V segment of width `reversal.w` around the `reversal.anchor` lobe (snakes/geometry.js:86-145)
- `centrelines` builds the strands as the spine offset by minus and plus R·u with u = cos θ N + sin θ B, with radius `a` scaled by the `swell` thickness profile, a `bead` factor on sin²θ, per-lobe Gaussian scales `lobes` and a head radius profile `heads`, with a shift near each neck that puts the head's snake on the spine while the partner snake wraps the neck (`wrap`, `neck`) (snakes/geometry.js:146-196)
- `centrelines` returns each strand head first, ends each tail at the point furthest behind the spine within `tail_hide` of the fitted end, scales the radius down to 0.75 over the last 30 px of each tail, and gives every sample a `mate`, `mateS` and `mateW` (the partner strand's point at the same station, the partner's arc length and a presence weight) (snakes/geometry.js:197-225)
- `strandSurface(strand, opts)` sweeps rings of `radial` = 64 vertices every `step` = 3 px along rotation-minimising frames (double reflection) after a Gaussian smoothing of the centreline by `smooth` samples, closes the ends with a superelliptic snout cap and a round tail cap, and blends ring vertices from a circle to a superellipse cross-section (`head.q`, `wide`, `flat`) by the per-sample `head` weight (snakes/geometry.js:264-279,287-292,362-414)
- Two corrections act on ring vertices inside `strandSurface`: the `fold` pinch limits radius times curvature towards each ring direction with a tanh knee between `fold[0]` and `fold[1]`, and the `press` flattens each strand against the plane halfway to the strand's `mate` with a soft knee of width `press.k` (snakes/geometry.js:321-340)
- `snakes/w-final.params.json` sets `tube` to `smooth` 4, `fold` [0.3, 0.5] and `press` {k 18, overlap 1.5} (snakes/w-final.params.json, key `tube`)
- `headSurface2` meshes each head and neck as one signed-distance field with surface nets at 2.5 px: the head field comes from swept profiles (upper, lower and depth outlines U, Lo and D as Catmull-Rom curves scaled by √(t(1−t)), two half-superellipses glued at the cheek line `beta`), the neck is a quintic Hermite curve from a point Q inside the head to the strand point at `join_s` matching position, tangent and curvature, and a polynomial smooth-minimum of width `neck_blend` unites the two fields (snakes/geometry.js:498,502-549,580-607,682-744)
- Both `head_profiles` of `snakes/w-final.params.json` set `field` to `sections`, which selects `headDistanceSections` — the minimum over stations of the axial offset combined with the in-plane distance to each planar section (snakes/geometry.js:609-654,727)
- `headSurface` (round cone plus neck) remains in `geometry.js` for `params.head_cones`; `snakes/w-final.params.json` holds both `head_cones` and `head_profiles`, and `createScene` takes the `head_profiles` branch first (snakes/scene.js:200-216; snakes/geometry.js:554-571)
- `tools/headmodel.py` repeats the head distance, the neck curve and the section-based distance in Python (`head_distance`, `neck_curve`, `head_distance_sections`) (tools/headmodel.py:1-18,51,96,163)
- `tools/bake_heads.mjs` meshes both heads for a params file and writes positions, normals and indices (base64) and the AO balls with a `params_hash` — `headKey` hashes the head profiles, necks, `neck_blend`, camera elevation and the strand samples from 20 px before to 60 px after `join_s` with FNV-1a, so body changes elsewhere leave the bake valid (tools/bake_heads.mjs:1-22; snakes/geometry.js:461-483)
- `unbake` returns null and logs a warning when the stored `params_hash` differs from `headKey`, after which `createScene` meshes the heads in the page (snakes/scene.js:162-172,200-208)
- `snakes/heads-final.json` (version 1) carries `params_hash` fdfd2035214abc54, which equals the `headKey` of `snakes/w-final.params.json` computed with `centrelines` of the same params (snakes/heads-final.json; snakes/geometry.js:464-472)
- `snakes/w-final.look.json` sets yellow `#ffc420`, blue `#0058f2`, `toneMapping` `aces`, `exposure` 0.95, `envLevel` 2.6 and a CSS blur `softness` of 0.9 px (snakes/w-final.look.json; defaults in `LOOK`, snakes/scene.js:10-26)
- `snakes/index.html` merges the look file over `LOOK` with `Object.assign`, which replaces nested objects such as `env` whole, and `snakes/still.html` merges a JSON `look` query parameter the same way (snakes/index.html:25; snakes/still.html:14-15)
- `studio` builds the lighting as a PMREM environment from a dim box room and feathered panels — a round key light at upper left, overhead, front, side fill, rim and floor — parameterised by `look.env` (snakes/scene.js:28-72)
- `createScene` gives each strand a `MeshPhysicalMaterial` with clear coat, and `withVertexAO` applies a per-vertex `ao` attribute to indirect diffuse, clear-coat and specular light (snakes/scene.js:76-95,248-263)
- `occlusion` computes `ao` per vertex from analytic balls along both strands (spacing `a`/2) and the ground plane, with an extra groove term near balls of the other strand (snakes/geometry.js:418-457; snakes/scene.js:228-247)
- `createScene` adds one sphere per eye, of radius `look.eyeR` (16.5 in `snakes/w-final.look.json`), sunk 0.35·`eyeR` below the head vertex whose image position lands nearest `look.eyes` ([369, 336] yellow, [1103, 324] blue in `LOOK`), with an optional `eyeSocket` AO band and a painted `catchlight` disc (snakes/scene.js:265-309; snakes/scene.js:18)
- The ground shadow is a transparent plane in `look.shadow.colour` whose alpha map holds soft blobs under every ball, and `look.shadowCard` — set in `snakes/w-final.look.json` — hides that plane in favour of a camera-facing card painted from the model's lowest point in each image column (snakes/scene.js:138-160,321-385)
- `snakes/index.html` fetches `w-final.params.json`, `w-final.look.json` and `heads-final.json` (query parameters `params`, `look`, `heads`; `heads=none` skips the bake), builds the scene, applies `look.softness` as a CSS blur and re-fits the canvas with a `ResizeObserver` (snakes/index.html:17-37)
- `snakes/still.html` renders a 1448 x 1086 canvas from the `params` query parameter, a JSON `look` query parameter and an optional `heads` file, supports `ss` supersampling and the diagnostic `yaw`, `pitch` and `pivot` rotation, and sets `window.__ready` (snakes/still.html:10-39)
- `tools/snake_still.mjs` renders `snakes/still.html` to a PNG in headless Chromium through Playwright (SwiftShader flags, `$CHROME` or a pre-installed binary), serves `snakes/` under the host `wend.local`, takes three r170 from `vendor/three.module.js` when present and from the CDN otherwise, waits for `window.__ready` and screenshots `#c` (tools/snake_still.mjs:1-41)
- `tools/snake_batch.mjs` renders a JSON list of jobs (`out`, `params`, `query`) to PNGs in one browser session (tools/snake_batch.mjs:1-41)
- `tools/check_page.mjs` loads any page of `snakes/` at a given viewport, logs console messages and page errors, and screenshots the page (tools/check_page.mjs:1-37)
- `tools/snakefit.py` holds the model used for fitting: `build` samples both snakes, `render_labels` splats balls with a z-buffer at scale 1/4 into a label image (0 background, 1 blue, 2 yellow), `ref_labels` derives the same labels from the reference by colour thresholds, `Loss` sums mean distance-transform distances and the mismatch percentage with an optional overlap-and-bend penalty, and the main block runs Powell passes over the `FREE` parameters (tools/snakefit.py:74,206-262,297-333,351-377)
- `tools/refine.py` (Powell over named parameters), `tools/profile.py` (rope width and centring along the spine), `tools/valleyfit.py` (anchors and spine offsets at the two valley bottoms), `tools/outlinefit.py` (spine offset knots against the reference outline in a window) and `tools/smooth_ends.py` (spine polyline near the necks and heads) refit subsets of the baseline parameters (docstrings of the five files)
- `tools/measure_ref.py` (colour masks, silhouette, medial axis), `tools/lobes.py` (spine from the medial axis and lobes from colour components, written to `reading.json`), `tools/direct.py` (params built from that reading with θ anchored at each lobe) and `tools/colourstats.py` (luminance percentiles and median colours of the blue and yellow parts) read the reference directly (docstrings of the four files)
- `tools/headref.py` (outline, open and hidden parts, landmarks, profiles and neck midline of each head, written to `headref.json`), `tools/headinit.py` (profile and neck parameters from `headref.json`), `tools/napefit.py`, `tools/nape.py`, `tools/nape2.py`, `tools/nape3.py` and `tools/rearcap.py` (rear head and upper neck fits and measurements), `tools/sections.py` and `tools/stageA.py` (cross-sections, curvature and head-field comparisons) and `tools/shading.py` (spherical-harmonic lighting fit to the reference luminance using model normals) cover the head and neck work (docstrings of the files)
- `tools/lobecmp.py` and `tools/lobecmp_lib.py` extract every visible lobe from the reference and from a render and compare centre, orientation, length, width and area; `tools/lobepred.py` predicts lobe centroid, orientation and size without rendering; `tools/lobetrack.py` compares the predicted change of each lobe with the change measured on renders (docstrings of the four files)
- `tools/compare.py` (side by side, 50% blend, label agreement), `tools/sbs.py`, `tools/look.py`, `tools/headsheet.py` and `tools/artsheet.py` write review sheets of the reference against renders (docstrings of the five files)
- `snakes/checkpoints/` holds 25 JSON files of earlier stages grouped in README.md:50-58; `checkpoints/final-art.json` and `checkpoints/look-final-art.json` are byte-identical to `snakes/w-final.params.json` and `snakes/w-final.look.json` (README.md:54)
- `snakes/stills/final.png` holds the committed render of the baseline and `snakes/stills/final-before-artpass.png` the render before the art-direction pass (README.md:4-5; progress/PROGRESS.md:3-4)
