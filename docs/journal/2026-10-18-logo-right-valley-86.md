- The crossing-order term of `tools/regionfit.py` keeps, for every non-adjacent pair whose screen distance at the start is below 0.9 of the radii sum and whose depth difference exceeds 15 px, the starting order with a 10 px margin (tools/regionfit.py:38-39,291-305,324-327)

- `tools/regionfit.py` minimises the weighted sum with SciPy L-BFGS-B on `jit(value_and_grad)` in x64 (`maxcor` 50, `maxiter` = `--iters`, `maxfun` = 2 × `--iters`), maps non-finite values to 1e30, writes a copy of the spec with the new control points and appends to the `regionfit` list of the spec a record of `box`, `args`, and the start and end values of every term, and with `--labels` writes a reference | prediction | disagreement PNG for the start and end states (tools/regionfit.py:359-410)
