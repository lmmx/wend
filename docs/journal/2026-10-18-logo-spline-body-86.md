## Current State

- `tools/jaxraster.py` `soft_labels` (JAX, x64) rasterises chains of balls with occlusion: per pixel and ball, coverage is sigmoid((r − |q − c|)/τ), background probability is the product of (1 − coverage), and colour is a softmax over all balls of log-coverage plus front-surface depth/γ, so two parts of one strand that overlap at a tight bend occlude each other (commit e6bc8ec; progress/PROGRESS.md:39-41)
- `tools/jaxsurface.py` as committed in e6bc8ec builds the swept surface the way the scene does — rotation-minimising frames by double reflection as a `lax.scan`, then rings — and measures `bend`, `jerk` and `fold` on the evaluated surface lines with differences divided by a fixed spacing `h`; the main block of the file compares autodiff with central differences (`git show e6bc8ec:logo/snake-w/tools/jaxsurface.py`; progress/PROGRESS.md:30-36,65)
- progress/PROGRESS.md records, for the e6bc8ec versions, a surface-fairness gradient relative error of 3e-8, an image-loss gradient relative error of 4e-6, and a cost of 0.4 s per value and gradient on a 16,800-px window with 435 balls (progress/PROGRESS.md:36,42-43)
- `tools/dump_strands.mjs` writes the `centrelines` strands of a params file, plus the tube centrelines the scene renders (strands sliced at each neck join, evened and smoothed by `strandSurface` with `fold` and `press` off), to JSON; with a spec argument the strands come from `strandsFromSpec` (tools/dump_strands.mjs:1-31)
- `tools/jaxraster_check.py` compares the raster's labels (`tau` 0.3, `gamma` 0.3) with the Three.js render's colour labels in four windows — left valley (420, 560, 700, 800), right valley (800, 540, 1080, 800), central arch (600, 380, 880, 620), left arm (380, 380, 520, 660) — as blue IoU, yellow IoU, silhouette IoU and pixel agreement (tools/jaxraster_check.py:1-29)
- progress/PROGRESS.md:49-52 records the e6bc8ec parity as blue IoU 0.873 / 0.897 / 0.859 / 0.894, yellow IoU 0.880 / 0.892 / 0.839 / 0.799, silhouette IoU 0.976 / 0.976 / 0.976 / 0.960 and pixel agreement 0.948 / 0.954 / 0.935 / 0.912 for the left valley, right valley, central arch and left arm, the left-arm window including the yellow neck that the raster does not model (progress/PROGRESS.md:45-52)
- `createScene` accepts a `body` option — `{ yellow, blue }` strands in the shape `centrelines` returns — which replaces the centreline model's strands while the spine, `L` and `theta` of `centrelines` remain (commit e6bc8ec; snakes/scene.js:174-177,193)
- `progress/PROGRESS.md` starts in e6bc8ec with the plan (one C2 representation per strand, fairness on the evaluated surface, JAX autodiff with L-BFGS-B, the Three.js render as judge), and `progress/00-baseline-sheet.png` shows reference, render before the art pass and baseline final (progress/PROGRESS.md:1-18)
- `tools/jaxsurface.py` after commit 709037f measures fairness per unit length: on every longitudinal surface line the curvature vector is Δ(unit tangent)/local arc length, `bend` is Σ|k|²·ds and `jerk` is Σ|Δk/ds|²·ds, each divided by the line length, and `spread` is the variance of the centreline sample spacing relative to the squared mean spacing (tools/jaxsurface.py:1-19,81-98)
- progress/PROGRESS.md:70 records a test of the same surface sampled three ways (spacing coefficient of variation 0.32, 0.54, 0.63) with bending within 0.4%, against a 4x variation for the fixed-`h` version, and a gradient relative error of 1.4e-8; the main block of `tools/jaxsurface.py` prints the gradient comparison and the uniform-versus-warped sampling comparison (tools/jaxsurface.py:101-129)
- `tools/jaxraster.py` after commit 709037f scores each ball in the colour softmax as `sharp`·log-coverage + z/γ with `sharp` = 20 by default — a ball nearer the camera that does not cover a pixel no longer wins the colour of that pixel, which removes colour bleed past the edge of a nearer disc (tools/jaxraster.py:11-15,28,39)
- progress/PROGRESS.md:75 records a two-ball test (front ball 40 px nearer) with the boundary 12–25 px off at `sharp` = 1 and 0.4–1.1 px off at `sharp` = 20, and progress/PROGRESS.md:81-84 records the parity after commit 709037f as blue IoU 0.944 / 0.944 / 0.951 / 0.926, yellow IoU 0.949 / 0.932 / 0.942 / 0.836, silhouette IoU 0.976 / 0.976 / 0.976 / 0.960 and pixel agreement 0.974 / 0.970 / 0.972 / 0.930 for the same four windows (progress/PROGRESS.md:72-86; image `progress/02-soft-raster-bleed-fix.png`)
- `tools/strandspline.py` and `snakes/strandspline.js` evaluate the spline body from a spec: per strand a clamped cubic B-spline centreline X(u) = Σ N_i(u) P_i in 3D and a cubic B-spline radius over the strand's own station u (yellow: spine station; blue: L − station), uniform clamped knots, with fields `s0`, `s1`, `nseg`, `ctrl`, `rseg`, `rctrl`; `arc_sample` and `arcSample` resample on a dense grid (24 points per span) to samples about `step` = 0.5 px apart in arc length (tools/strandspline.py:1-48,78-99; snakes/strandspline.js:1-88)
- `strands(spec)` in `tools/strandspline.py` and `strandsFromSpec(spec)` in `snakes/strandspline.js` return the fields the scene consumes (`pts`, `r`, `s`, `head`, `mate`, `mateS`, `mateW`); the mate is the partner at the same shared station by default (`spec.mate` = `station`) or the closest partner sample within `mate_window` stations (`closest`), and `mateW` ramps over 25 px before the partner's tail end (tools/strandspline.py:127-153; snakes/strandspline.js:92-124)
- progress/PROGRESS.md:96 records the JS and NumPy evaluators agreeing to 1e-11 px, and the join point within 0.3 px and tangent within 1.6–3.2 degrees of the baseline's at the head joins (progress/PROGRESS.md:96,98)
- `tools/fit_splines.py` fits a spec to the baseline strands that `tools/dump_strands.mjs` dumps: per strand, a weighted least squares linear in the control points, minimising Σ w|Bc − P|² ds + lam3 ∫|X'''|² + lam2 ∫|X''|² for the centreline and mu2 ∫r''² + mu3 ∫r'''² for the radius, with weights raised on the neck windows (`join_s` − 40 to `join_s` + 60) and the tail ends (tools/fit_splines.py:1-16,104-144)
- `tools/fit_splines.py` option `tight` raises the fairness weight, and lowers the data weight, in a band of `tight_span` px around samples whose r·κ exceeds `tight_from`, iterated `iters` times, and option `jump` gives a window of `jump_w` px around each jump of the baseline strands (speed above `jump` times the median) a data weight of 0.01 (tools/fit_splines.py:104-144)
- `tools/fit_splines.py` takes `[params.json] [--sweep] [--write tag:h:lam3:mu2[:key=value,...]]`, prints deviation from the baseline strands, fairness (curvature, r·κ, radius derivative) and strand-contact overlap statistics, writes `snakes/spline-<tag>.json` for `--write` configurations and writes `arch/fit-metrics.json` and `arch/fit-profiles.npy` (tools/fit_splines.py:14-15,203-245)
- `snakes/spline-first.json` and `snakes/spline-lo.json` carry identical content: `fit` h 16, lam3 1e4, mu2 2000, w_neck 2000, rseg_h 12, tight 100, tight_from 3, iters 2, `tube` `{smooth: 0}`, 84 yellow and 85 blue centreline control points, 111 and 113 radius control points (snakes/spline-first.json, snakes/spline-lo.json)
- `snakes/spline-hi.json` differs from `spline-lo.json` in `fit` (lam3 1e5, mu2 5000); `snakes/spline-lo-nofold.json` equals `spline-lo.json` except `tube` = `{smooth: 0, fold: null}`; `snakes/spline-nocusp.json` has the `fit` entries of `spline-lo.json` without `tight`, `tight_from` and `iters` and different control points (snakes/spline-hi.json, snakes/spline-lo-nofold.json, snakes/spline-nocusp.json)
- `snakes/still-spline.html` extends `still.html` with a `spec` query parameter: the page fetches the spec, merges `spec.tube` over `params.tube` (so `smooth: 0` and `fold: null` replace the baseline values while the baseline `press` stays), converts the spec with `strandsFromSpec` and passes the result as `body` to `createScene` (snakes/still-spline.html:17-26)
- `tools/spline_batch.mjs` renders a JSON list of jobs (`out`, `params`, `query`, optional `page`, default `still-spline.html`) to PNGs in one browser session (tools/spline_batch.mjs:1-43)
- `snakes/diag-clay.html` builds the scene as `still.html` does, swaps every mesh material per mode — `clay` (matte grey under three directional lights and a hemisphere light), `zebra` and `zebrav` (mirror material in a PMREM environment of parallel bright bars about world up or world x), `curv` (screen-space |dN|/|dP| of the interpolated normal times 40 px as a colour ramp) — renders each mode at each view (full frame or crop boxes in reference pixels) with supersampling `ss` (default 2) and crop scale `cs` (default 3), and returns PNG data URLs in `window.__shots` (snakes/diag-clay.html:4-20,72-166)
