## 2. Global findings

- Bounding box matches (x 48–331 fp; y 61–224 vs 61–222). Foreground area ~4.6% larger (yellow ~9% more, blue ~4% more). Both valley bottoms 2–6 fp too high.
- Left V, right V and arch counter match within 1–3 fp from y≈95 to 190; below that the arch counter's left foot is 3–5 fp narrower (left-valley yellow pushes in).
- Arch: silhouette and colour sequence correct; internal errors (L07 runs on as a band, L08 flattened, L09's tail short, pinch dimples at L08/L09/L10).
- Heads: within ~1–2 fp; mouth lines missing; the blue wrap rises ~8 fp too high behind the yellow head.
- **Sculptural character:** the reference reads as stacked compact convex inflated cushions/rings tilted ~30–35°, crisp glints, deep dark rolled crevices, distinct round balls at the valley bottoms. The candidate reads as a two-strand twisted rope: arm lobes 15–20° steeper, elongated lenses with pointed ends; zebra S-swirls (saddle-shaped twisted surfaces) on L01–L03, L10–L11, L15–L16 instead of concentric ovals; the valleys built from crossing tubes with hard intersection seams, notches and folds.
- Material: weaker, broader highlights (satin, not glossy); lit yellow lemon-tinted; softer image; weaker contact shadows; sharper, lower horizon seam.

4. L04/L06: the left-valley yellow is one fused oversized bent tube instead of two round lobes divided by a crevice.
5. L01 climbs ~8 fp too high behind the yellow head.
