## 2. Global findings

- Overall W proportions very close: bounding box identical horizontally, nearly so vertically; silhouette area within 1.4%; the errors are inside the silhouette.
- The left and right notches and the opening under the arch match to within a few px.
- Valley bottoms are wrong: the left valley rests on one yellow bulb instead of a yellow and blue pair; the right valley's bottom sits ~18 px above the reference ground line.
- Arch: the dome and the right leg are good; the left leg (L06/L07) has flattened, ribbon-like lobes with a concave crease and straight ledges.
- Heads within ~1 whole-frame px; on both necks the first lobe of the other colour (L01, L16) climbs ~25 px too high.
- The arms read as compact inflated twisted lobes like the reference; the two valley bottoms and the arch's left leg read as a lumpy, partly merged knot.
- Rendering (not geometry): blurrier, broader highlights; yellow more lemon; a darker hard-edged horizon band at the base line.

## 3. Five most damaging discrepancies

1. L05: the left valley's bottom blue lobe is half hidden behind an oversized L04 bulb and cut into a wedge.
2. L13: the right valley's bottom blue lobe is a notched, chevron-shaped kidney with a fold crease and a zebra pinch, in front of its neighbours when it should be behind them.
3. L14: about 40% of the big rising yellow lobe in the right valley is rendered blue by L13's upward bulge.
4. L06/L07: the arch's left leg is a flattened, creased, ribbon-like bend, and L06 merges into L04 with no crevice.
5. L04: about 28% too wide and too round at the base, running ~45 px too far right; the right valley also floats ~18 px above the floor.
