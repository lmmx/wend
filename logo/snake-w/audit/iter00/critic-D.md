## 2. Global findings

- Overall extent and head placement match closely; the error is in the lobes, not the layout.
- The reference's bottom lobes reach the floor; in the candidate the valley-bottom lobes (L05, L13) are hidden or truncated.
- The candidate's grooves are shallower and smoother than the reference's V-notches and arch opening.
- The arch's parts are elongated (L07) or wedge-shaped (L08) rather than plump; L09's lower tail is cut off.
- Left valley: yellow (L04+L06) merges into one mass and the blue L05 is nearly lost. Right valley: L13 is boxy and pinched.
- Sculptural character: the reference reads as fat inflated balloon lobes with deep grooves; the candidate reads as a uniform twisted rope or cable: roughly constant diameter, shallow grooves, lobes longer, thinner and more angular.
- Surface: mostly concentric zebra rings, with vortex points at L13/L14 and stripe breaks at the L04, L06 and L08 junctions; the eyes are stuck-on spheres in clay.

## 3. Five most damaging discrepancies

1. The valley-bottom blue lobes (L05, L13) are boxy, angular and do not reach the floor, and L13 has a visible pinch.
2. The left-valley yellow mass (L04+L06) merges into one flat spread with a slit, a hard vertical blue wedge and a pointed lower right.
3. Overall the candidate reads as a thin, uniform rope with shallow grooves rather than fat inflated lobes, most visibly in the arm lobes (L02, L15, L16).
4. L03 is truncated by an overextended yellow lobe, and L09's lower tail is cut, so the over/under order is wrong in two places.
5. The arch lobes L07 and L08 are elongated and wedge-shaped with sharp corners, not plump round ellipses and shells.
